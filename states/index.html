Bars count seats toward the majority line: seats not up this year count for the party that holds them, then called races. A chamber is decided once one party's held and won seats reach the majority. Hatched segments are uncalled districts where a party leads with at least 60% of the expected vote counted; they show where the count is heading and never count toward control.
