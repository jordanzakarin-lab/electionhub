Covers every governor, lieutenant governor, attorney general, secretary of state, treasurer (including Florida's chief financial officer) and auditor or comptroller elected statewide on Nov. 3, 2026. Offices that are appointed, chosen by the legislature or elected in other years are listed under each state in the state view. Where the lieutenant governor runs on a joint ticket with the governor, the row says so. Flip: the winner's party differs from the party of the current officeholder. Last margin: the winning party's lead over the runner-up, in percentage points, the last time the office was on the ballot. Margin: Democratic share minus Republican share. Results: official state results sites first, then AP and Decision Desk HQ calls as reported. Races update about every 15 minutes on election night until 3am ET, then less often through Nov. 6. A race is marked called only when AP, Decision Desk HQ or a major network calls it. Each race shows when its numbers were last updated.
